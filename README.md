## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 01 · Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis

**Python · Pandas · Matplotlib**

An exploratory data analysis of approximately **51,700 Bengaluru restaurant listings**, investigating restaurant distribution, customer ratings, pricing and service preferences.

**What I worked on:**

- Inspected the dataset structure, column types, missing values and duplicate records.
- Selected relevant analytical fields and removed columns outside the scope of structured analysis.
- Standardised inconsistent restaurant-rating values and converted approximate-cost fields into numerical formats.
- Examined missing data and removed duplicate records to improve data quality.
- Used Pandas frequency analysis, filtering, grouping, aggregation and descriptive statistics to investigate restaurant characteristics.
- Explored differences in average ratings and costs across online-ordering and table-booking groups.
- Created Matplotlib visualisations to examine restaurant concentration across locations and the distribution of restaurant ratings.

**Key analytical areas:**
- Restaurant concentration by neighbourhood.
- Distribution of customer ratings.
- Restaurant types and popularity patterns.
- Pricing characteristics.
- Service availability and comparative analysis.

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook.
